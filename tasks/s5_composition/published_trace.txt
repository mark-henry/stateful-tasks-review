NOTE: neither of this task's originally-assigned primary sources (Liu et al. 2022,
arXiv:2210.10749; Barrington 1989; Merrill & Sabharwal 2023, arXiv:2310.07923) publishes a
textual CoT/scratchpad trace of the S5 word problem at all -- Liu et al.'s transformers consume
the automaton as direct categorical/numeric input for final-state classification (no natural
language, no intermediate text), and the other two are pure theory papers with no example
instances. This trace is the closest available PUBLISHED, code-verified, textual rendering of the
identical S_n word-problem construction (same group, same one-line-notation composition), from a
follow-up paper/repo that trains actual language models (GPT-2/Pythia) on it as text.
